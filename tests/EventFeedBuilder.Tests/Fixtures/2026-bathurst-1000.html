The Great Race

Get ready for the Repco Bathurst 1000 when it returns to Mount Panorama, October 8 - 11! Australia’s most iconic endurance race returns for another epic chapter, with the Ryco Enduro Cup continuing to raise the stakes across the Supercars calendar. Who will the mountain choose as its conqueror in 2026?

Following one of the most Unforgettable races of 2025, this is a must-attend event for motorsport fans of all generations. Off the track, enjoy live music, family-friendly entertainment, historic car displays, and plenty of food and merch.

With General Admission or Grandstand tickets to choose from, there is an option for everyone - and remember, kids 12 and under get in free with a paying adult
